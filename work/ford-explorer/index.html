Ford Explorer™ CX740S

By far one of my best experiences at Digital Salmon, built in collaboration with Imagination. I was responsible for modelling and texturing this vehicle for a real-time, pixel-streamed experience in UE5 for Ford™.

I also helped lead the modelling team throughout. It was my first time leading others, and helping to build and then manage a pipeline. It was a tough challenge, and I could not be more proud of what we achieved.

Role
Vehicle modelling and texturing, modelling team lead
Context
Digital Salmon, with Imagination
Tools
Unreal Engine, Maya, Houdini, Substance 3D Designer, Substance 3D Painter
